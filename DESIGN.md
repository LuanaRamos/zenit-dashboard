# Design System

## Theme

**Base Style:** Modern Dark Admin Dashboard
**Vibe:** Professional, Sleek, Data-Heavy, High-Contrast

## Colors

- **Primary:** `#FDBA21` (Zenit Gold/Yellow)
- **Primary Gradient:** `#FDBA21` to `#FFD54F`
- **Background:** `#0A0A0A` (Deep Black)
- **Surface (Cards/Sidebar):** `#141414` (Elevated Dark)
- **Text Primary:** `#FFFFFF`
- **Text Secondary:** `#A1A1AA`
- **Success:** `#25D366` (WhatsApp Green)
- **Border:** `#27272A`

## Typography

- **Font Family:** Inter / Sans-Serif
- **Headings:** Bold, clean, minimal letter spacing.
- **Body:** 14px-16px, readable.

## Layout & Components

- **Sidebar:** Dark surface (`#141414`), clean navigation links sem o estilo padrão do Streamlit.
- **Metric Cards:** Cantos arredondados (8px), bordas sutis (`#27272A`), sem sombras duras, cor de destaque nos valores.
- **Tables:** Bordas minimalistas, cabeçalhos discretos.
- **Charts:** Gráficos Plotly com fundos transparentes (`rgba(0,0,0,0)`), linhas de grade quase invisíveis (`#2A2A2A`).

## Anti-Patterns
- Tema claro (Light mode).
- Estilo padrão do Streamlit.
- Cards brancos saltando na tela.
